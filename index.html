Madagascar

Madagascar, the world’s fourth-largest island located off the coast of East Africa, is a renowned biodiversity hotspot, recognized for its unique, isolated evolution. Over 90% of its wildlife—including lemurs and baobabs—is endemic. The country features varied landscapes, from eastern rainforests to western dry forests, and a unique culture influenced by Indonesian and African roots.
