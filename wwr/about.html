About Us - Rapids

Rapids wasn’t founded in a boardroom; it was born in the middle of a Class IV swell. What started as a group of friends chasing the perfect line turned into a mission: to share the raw, unbridled energy of the river with anyone brave enough to pick up a paddle. At Rapids, we believe the river has a way of washing away the everyday and replacing it with something legendary.

History

Rapids didn’t start with a business plan; it started with a flooded basement and a borrowed school bus. Our founder, "River" Rick Miller, was a seasonal carpenter who spent his winters dreaming of the spring thaw. In the May of ’84, after a record-breaking snowpack melted into the valley, Rick and three friends shoved a patched-up military surplus raft into the churning grey water of the Devil’s Throat. They didn't have GPS, high-tech Gore-Tex, or even matching paddles. What they had was a cooler of sandwiches, a heavy wooden oar that had seen better days, and a realization: The river was where they felt most alive.
